## 1. Outcome roadmap

_A multi-team roadmap organized by **outcomes**, not feature lists. Near-term work protects trust and removes barriers to retention, while the next horizons test and scale Fable’s longer-term resilience strategy._

| Horizon | Outcome / bet | Owning team(s) | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now (0 to 3 mo) | **We bet delivering notifications at the right local time will increase meaningful re-engagement for post-acute users without creating notification fatigue.** | Toolkit & Re-entry squad; Engineering | Meaningful reactivation increases from baseline while notification opt-outs and trust scores do not decline. |
| Now (0 to 3 mo) | **We bet reducing app load time will increase returning-session completion for lapsed deep engagers.** | Toolkit & Re-entry squad; Engineering | App load time falls below two seconds on 4G and returning-session completion improves. |
| Now (0 to 3 mo) | **We bet removing accessibility barriers will improve continued use for users who rely on accessibility features.** | Engineering; Design; Legal | Critical WCAG 2.1 gaps are closed and completion rates improve for users relying on accessibility features. |
| Next (3 to 6 mo) | **Prove that structured resilience practices help post-acute users build lasting skills and remain subscribed.** | Progression squad; Content; Clinical advisory | Month-six premium retention for the test cohort improves by 15 percentage points over baseline or control. |
| Next (3 to 6 mo) | **Help more users feel capable of handling a stressful week using skills learned through Fable.** | Progression squad; Clinical advisory; Research | The share of the full cohort reporting greater ability to manage stress increases by 20 percentage points at day 150. |
| Next (3 to 6 mo) | **Give lapsed users a personally relevant reason to return when stress reappears.** | Toolkit & Re-entry squad; Research; Analytics | Meaningful reactivation among lapsed deep engagers increases toward the target of twice the current baseline. |
| Next (3 to 6 mo) | **Recognize the post-acute transition early enough to offer a useful next step.** | Toolkit & Re-entry squad; Analytics | More eligible users move into continued skill-building without increasing opt-outs, complaints or unhealthy engagement. |
| Next (3 to 6 mo) | **Demonstrate that post-acute value improves paid renewal, not just short-term engagement.** | Product; Growth; Finance; Research | Premium renewal improves against control and users show evidence of willingness to continue paying for the resilience experience. |
| Later (6 to 12 mo) | **Bet: Expand the resilience-track portfolio once the first tracks demonstrate measurable retention and customer outcomes.** | Progression squad; Content; Clinical advisory | Retention and coping improvements can be repeated across multiple validated tracks. |
| Later (6 to 12 mo) | **Bet: Introduce adaptive personalization only if it produces incremental value beyond simple rules.** | Both squads; Engineering; Data | Adaptive recommendations outperform rules-based targeting on retention, reactivation or customer outcomes. |
| Later (6 to 12 mo) | **Bet: Build a longer-term resilience history that helps users understand what works across different stressful periods.** | Toolkit & Re-entry squad; Research | Repeat returning users report greater preparedness and make continued use of their personal toolkit. |
| Later (6 to 12 mo) | **Bet: Explore wearable access if it makes Fable more useful in relevant moments rather than simply increasing activity.** | Toolkit & Re-entry squad; Engineering; Partnerships | Wearable users show improved meaningful practice or reactivation compared with similar mobile-only users. |
| Later (6 to 12 mo) | **Bet: Expand into additional languages and markets only after the retention model is proven in the US, UK and Canada.** | Product; Content; Localization; Growth | A new market can reproduce the retention improvement with sustainable subscription economics. |
