# Team Charter: [Fable / Meridian / your initiative]

> Module 3 · Lead and Develop High-Performing Teams, ★ Deliverable 3
>
> Define how your team operates. Two completed components: **What We Own** and **How We Decide**.

## 1. What We Own

_The team's mandate: the outcomes and surfaces this team is accountable for end-to-end, and the explicit edges where our ownership stops._

| Area | We own it | We influence it (don't own) |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile customer journey | Onboarding, post-acute retention and re-entry for premium and trial users in the US, UK and Canada | New-user acquisition, web, non-English markets and B2B |
| Onboarding | PM 1 owns the onboarding experience and activation outcomes | AI check-in capability and lifecycle campaigns |
| Retention and re-entry | PM 2 owns skill tracks, the personal toolkit, renewal and reactivation outcomes | Content production, clinical guidance and lifecycle campaigns |
| AI check-ins | PM 3 owns prompts, personalization logic, reliability and AI guardrails | Placement within onboarding and retention, which remains with the surface PM |
| Growth campaigns | The Growth Lead owns campaign execution, conversion and renewal offers | In-product journeys, which remain with the surface PM |
| Safety and compliance | We own implementing approved requirements in the product | Clinical and Legal own final approval of claims, sensitive-data use and safety boundaries |

Out of scope this year: clinical treatment or therapist matching, community features, B2B, web, non-English markets, new-user acquisition, and streak- or guilt-based engagement.

> Our mission in one line: Help deeply engaged users move from short-term relief to lasting resilience, giving them a reason to stay with Fable and return when they need it.

## 2. How We Decide

_The team's decision-making operating model: the decisions we make, who makes each call, and how disagreements resolve._

| Decision type | Who decides | Who's consulted | How we break a tie |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feature and scope within onboarding | PM 1 | Design, Engineering, Growth and PM 3 when AI is involved | Product Leader decides within two business days |
| Feature and scope within retention or re-entry | PM 2 | Design, Engineering, Content, Clinical and PM 3 when AI is involved | Product Leader decides within two business days |
| AI capability and behaviour | PM 3 | Surface PM, Engineering, Data, Clinical and Legal | Product Leader resolves product trade-offs; Engineering resolves reliability or security issues |
| Growth experiment affecting an in-product journey | Surface PM and Growth Lead jointly | Design, Engineering, Analytics and PM 3 when AI is involved | Product Leader decides within two business days |
| Roadmap, OKRs, target segment or cross-team priority | Product Leader | PMs, Growth, Engineering, Clinical and Finance | Executive sponsor decides if the change affects board-approved strategy, funding or the business model |
| Clinical, safety or regulatory decision | Clinical and Legal | Product Leader, relevant PM and Engineering | Clinical and Legal have final authority |

When owners disagree, they document the options, evidence, OKR impact and risks, then meet within two business days. Unresolved issues are escalated to the Product Leader within one business day.

> Our default: The PM who owns the product surface decides; we escalate to the Product Leader when a decision crosses team boundaries or the owners cannot agree.
