# Financial Model: Fable Team Tier

> Module 5 · Master Product Financials & Strategic Bets, ★ Deliverable 5
>
> The business case for funding the Fable Team Tier and the explicit criteria that would tell us to stop.

## 1. Business case

_Why this initiative is worth funding over the alternatives. The model uses gross profit rather than revenue when calculating LTV and payback._

| Assumption | Value | Source / rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $12 per seat per month | Proposed price for SMB wellness teams |
| Average team size | 6 paid seats | Planning assumption to validate during the pilot |
| Conversion rate | 4% | Expected conversion among verified SMB wellness decision-makers |
| Gross margin | 85% | Planning assumption that Finance must confirm |
| Annual churn | 15% | Expected annual team churn |
| CAC | $950 per team | $38 individual CAC ÷ 4% conversion |
| LTV | $4,896 per team | $864 ARR × 85% gross margin ÷ 15% annual churn |
| LTV:CAC | 5.15:1 | $4,896 gross-profit LTV ÷ $950 team CAC |
| Payback period | 15.5 months | $950 CAC ÷ $61.20 monthly gross profit |
| Investment required | At least $114,000 | $38 CAC allocated across 3,000 qualified users; incremental product and operating costs must be added before final approval |
| Expected return | $103,680 exit ARR | 120 converted teams × $864 ARR |
| Expected gross profit | $88,128 annualized | $103,680 ARR × 85% gross margin |

> **The case in one paragraph:** Fable will pilot a team tier with 3,000 existing users who are verified SMB wellness decision-makers. At a 4% conversion rate, the pilot will generate 120 paid teams, 720 seats, $8,640 in exit MRR and $103,680 in exit ARR. At an 85% gross margin, each team produces $734.40 in annual gross profit, with a 15.5-month payback period and a 5.15:1 LTV:CAC ratio. If churn rises from 15% to 25%, LTV:CAC remains slightly above the required level at 3.09:1.

## 2. Kill criteria

_The specific signals that would tell us this bet is no longer worth pursuing._

> If paid individual-to-team conversion does not reach **3.9% after exposure to 3,000 verified SMB decision-makers by the end of Q2**, or fully loaded team CAC exceeds **$1,102**, we will stop further investment, commit no Q3 headcount and return the remaining funding to the core B2C retention roadmap.

The 3.9% conversion threshold protects a minimum 3:1 gross-profit LTV:CAC ratio if annual churn increases from 15% to 25%. The $1,102 CAC ceiling represents the maximum cost that supports an 18-month gross-margin payback.
